## 2. HAS A PROBLEM

### THE VILLAIN

Other AI coding platforms (lovable.dev, bolt.new, v0.dev) promise rapid development but abandon you halfway. They generate beautiful demos that crumble when you try to launch a real business.

### External Problem (What They Can See)
- **Broken authentication** that works in dev but fails in production
- **Missing payment infrastructure** - no Stripe integration, no subscriptions
- **No backend** or database architecture for real user data
- **Security vulnerabilities** in 45% of AI-generated code
- **Deployment nightmares** - "works on my machine" but not in production
- **$68K-$132K hidden costs** to make AI prototypes production-ready

### Internal Problem (How It Makes Them Feel)
- **Frustrated**: "I spent months building, but can't launch"
- **Betrayed**: "The AI promised a finished app, but it's only 60-70% done"
- **Anxious**: "Is my app secure? Will payments work? What did the AI miss?"
- **Overwhelmed**: "I need to learn DevOps, security, and Stripe just to launch?"
- **Embarrassed**: "I told everyone I'd launch this month..."

### Philosophical Problem (Why It's Just Plain Wrong)
**AI coding tools should eliminate the 65-75% of work that ISN'T coding** - the infrastructure, security, payments, monitoring, and deployment. But current platforms only handle the easy 25% (generating code) and leave you with the hard 75%.

**This is backwards.** The promise of AI is to handle complexity, not multiply it.
