Under the hood

For the curious: how it builds a real app

You never have to read this part — but if you want to know what's happening under the hood, here it is. Dreamforge turns a plain-language idea into a working, published app through a careful, step-by-step process rather than a single one-shot guess. It writes a plan, builds the app in reviewable stages, and runs automated checks — testing and fixing problems before you ever see them.

The behind-the-scenes part is real. Dreamforge sets up and connects Cloudflare's own building blocks — a real SQL database (D1), file storage (R2), stateful services (Durable Objects), and fast key-value storage (KV) — as one integrated system. There's no separate database company to sign up for, no glue code between services, and no surprise data-transfer fees.

When it's ready, it publishes to Cloudflare's global network in one click. The result is a genuine, complete product on professional infrastructure — and the code is standard React and Cloudflare Workers, so a developer can read it, export it, and own it.

The pipeline

  • Blueprint — your idea becomes a clear plan
  • Step-by-step build — code created in reviewable stages
  • Automatic checks — testing and validation as it goes
  • Self-healing — it finds and fixes its own errors
  • Real data layer — D1, R2, Durable Objects, KV
  • One-click publish — live on Cloudflare's edge

Questions

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Describe what you want in everyday language and Dreamforge builds it. If you do happen to know how to code, even better — you can open up the app and change anything.

What if I'm not sure how to describe my idea?

That's completely normal — most people don't know exactly what to ask for. Dreamforge asks helpful questions and fills in the parts you wouldn't think to mention, so a rough idea is enough to get a real app.

How is this different from Lovable, Bolt, or v0?

Those tools are great at producing the look of an app. Dreamforge builds the whole thing — everything behind the scenes too — in one connected place, and puts it live on a worldwide network in one click. A real app, not just a demo.

Is it a real app, or just a preview?

Real. Your app has real sign-ins, a real database, and real file storage, and it's live on the internet with its own link. It handles real visitors and real data from the moment it launches.

Do I own it, or am I locked in?

You own it. Your app is built on open, standard technology, and you can export it and take it anywhere — hand it to a developer, or keep building with us. There's no black box and nothing holding it hostage.

What will it cost to keep my app running?

Running an app on Cloudflare is genuinely affordable — generous free limits and fair, predictable pricing. Most early apps run for just a few dollars a month and grow smoothly, without the surprise bills common to other tools.

What if it builds something wrong?

Dreamforge checks its own work as it goes — testing and fixing problems before you see them — and you can ask for changes anytime and watch it update live. It improves until it's right, rather than guessing once and hoping.

Can I bring a project I've already started?

Yes. Dreamforge can pick up an existing project and keep building on it, so you can bring work you've already started and take it the rest of the way to live.

What are Sparks?

Sparks are the simple credits that power your builds — and every price is published: building a complete app is 200, a change is 30, putting it live is 10. You get 300 free when you sign up and 150 more every month. No meters you can't read, no bill you can't predict.

What's the difference between Explore and Produce?

Explore is where you build with AI yourself — free to start, powered by Sparks. Produce is where we take it the rest of the way with you: demand validated first, built by an assigned Dreamforge engineer, shipped live, and kept alive after launch.

Do I have to explore before you'll produce?

No — you can apply to produce on day one. But exploring first helps: a working app you've already touched makes the scoping call sharper and the build faster.

What does the Traction Sprint guarantee?

Our guarantee is simple: you will never spend the build cost discovering the market didn't want it. The $750 Sprint proves real demand before the build begins — and if the market says no, that's the cheapest lesson in business, not a failed project.
