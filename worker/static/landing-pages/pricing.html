Questions

Pricing questions, answered

What are Sparks, in plain English?

The simple credits that power your builds. Building a complete app is 200, a change is 30, an image is 65, going live is 10 — published right here, always. A built, launched, polished app is usually 600–700 Sparks.

Do unused Sparks roll over?

On paid plans, yes — unused Sparks stay good for one extra month past your billing period. The free monthly 150 resets each month, and your 300 welcome Sparks never expire.

What does the Produce monthly cover?

Keeping your product alive: hosting, monitoring, updates, ongoing engineer hours, and team training. The one-time fee is the build; the monthly is the run.

What happens on the scoping call?

A real conversation about what you're building and who it's for. You leave with a fixed price in writing and a clear plan — and only then, if you want to proceed, a payment link. No pressure, no obligation.

What if the Traction Sprint says there's no demand?

Then you just saved your entire build budget for $750 — and you'll know exactly why, with real signals, not guesses. Most people redirect that budget to the next idea. That's not a failed project; that's the cheapest lesson in business.

Can I start with Explore and move to Produce later?

That's the whole path. Explore free, build something real, and when it's time to put it in front of real customers, we produce it with you — no rebuild, no starting over. Everything you explored comes with you.
