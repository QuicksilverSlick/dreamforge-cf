## Conclusion

### Key Takeaways

1. **Strong Unit Economics**: LTV:CAC ratios improve from 5.6:1 to 12.0:1 by Year 3
2. **High Gross Margins**: Consistent 85% gross margins across all years
3. **Scalable Growth**: 275% CAGR over 3 years
4. **Balanced Revenue Mix**: Shifts from 73/27 (B2B/B2C) to 54/46 by Year 3
5. **VC-Fundable Scale**: $55M ARR by Year 3 supports $200M+ Series B valuation

### Risk Factors

1. **Churn Sensitivity**: 1% change in churn = ±11.6% revenue variance
2. **Conversion Dependency**: Success relies on 6% conversion by Year 3
3. **Team Expansion**: Business model depends on average team size growth
4. **Competition**: Market saturation could impact conversion and retention
5. **AI Costs**: Rising AI API costs could compress margins

### Recommendations

1. **Focus on Retention**: Reduce churn from 5% to 3% in Year 1
2. **Optimize Onboarding**: Improve free-to-paid conversion to 6%+
3. **Upsell Strategy**: Increase average team size in business segment
4. **Annual Push**: Increase annual subscriptions to 60%+ (improves cash flow)
5. **Series A Timing**: Raise when ARR > $5M (Q4 Year 1 or Q1 Year 2)
