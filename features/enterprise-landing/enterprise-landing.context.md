# Enterprise Landing Page Feature Context
**Created:** 2025-10-29
**Purpose:** B2B landing page targeting businesses for AI upskilling training
**Framework:** StoryBrand 2.0
**Size:** ~1.8KB

## Quick Overview
Complete landing page package selling Dreamforge Enterprise Training ($297/mo BETA) to small businesses (1-50 employees) who want to upskill 1-3 staff members to build AI-powered apps.

## Key Files
- `index.html` (main landing page)
- `styles.css` (design system, color scheme)
- `script.js` (ROI calculator, analytics, forms)
- `lead-magnet-outline.md` (10-page PDF guide structure)
- `email-sequence.md` (3 nurture + 1 sales emails)
- `README.md` (complete implementation guide)

## Target Customer
**ICP:** Small business owner (5-50 employees, $500K-$10M revenue)
**Pain:** Can't afford developers, drowning in SaaS costs, feeling left behind by AI
**Goal:** Upskill team to build custom apps, save $50K+/year
**Objections:** "My team isn't technical," "Too expensive," "No time"

## Competitive Positioning
**vs. Lovable:** Open source, multi-LLM, training included
**vs. Bolt.new:** Cloudflare native, quality checks, no lock-in
**vs. V0:** Multi-LLM, backend preview, enterprise training
**Unique:** Only platform with hands-on training + open source + multi-LLM

## Pricing Strategy
- **BETA:** $297/mo (40% off regular $497)
- **Additional seats:** $97/mo (4-10 people: $87, 11-20: $77)
- **Psychological tactics:** Charm pricing, anchoring, loss aversion, volume discounts
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back

## StoryBrand Elements
1. **Hero:** Small business owner/manager
2. **Problem:** Can't afford devs (external), feel overwhelmed (internal), unfair disadvantage (philosophical)
3. **Guide:** Dreamforge (empathy + Cloudflare authority)
4. **Plan:** 3 steps (Start training → Build apps → Scale independently)
5. **CTA:** Direct (Start BETA) + Transitional (Free guide)
6. **Stakes:** Competitors winning, costs rising, talent leaving
7. **Success:** $50K savings, 3x efficiency, competitive advantage
