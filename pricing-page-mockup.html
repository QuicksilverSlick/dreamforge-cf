Pricing Questions

Why is Dreamforge more expensive than Bolt.new or v0?

Great question! While Bolt.new ($20) and v0 ($20) focus on basic code generation, Dreamforge includes:

  • Live training hours — 1-on-1 coaching with AI experts (worth $100+/hour alone)
  • Advanced AI models — Claude 4 Sonnet, GPT-4, specialized for production apps
  • Phase-wise generation — Built for complex, real-world applications
  • Production-ready code — Not just prototypes

Think of it as Bolt.new + $150/month in training = $170 value for just $27.

What happens if I exceed my training hours?

Pro includes 10 hours/month, Ultimate includes 20 hours. If you need more:

  • Additional hours: $15/hour (cheaper than upgrading tiers)
  • OR upgrade to Ultimate for better per-hour value
  • Unused hours don't roll over (use it or lose it)

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! Upgrade anytime and pay the difference. Downgrade at next billing cycle. Annual plans can be upgraded mid-year (you'll be credited the difference).

Do you offer student or nonprofit discounts?

Yes! Students and nonprofits get 50% off Pro and Ultimate plans. Email sales@dreamforge.dev with proof of status (.edu email or 501(c)(3) documentation).
