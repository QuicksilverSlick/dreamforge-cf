## Recommended Pricing Structure

### Individual/Vibe Coder Segment

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (16.7% off) | Key Features |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 5 projects/mo, public deployments, watermark |
| **Pro** (MOST POPULAR) | **$27** | **$22.47**/mo ($269/year) | Unlimited projects, 10 training hours/mo |

**Why $27 (Not $20 Like Competitors)?**
- **Differentiation**: Training hours + advanced AI models justify premium
- **Positioning**: Higher price signals higher quality
- **Research**: Charm pricing ($27) outperforms $25 by 8-12%
- **Value**: $27 platform + $150/mo training value = $177 total value

### Business/Enterprise Training Segment

| Team Size | Monthly | Annual (16.7% off) | Per Seat/Mo | Discount |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|-------------|----------|

**Volume Discount Strategy:**
- **Aggressive First Discount**: 39% off when adding 2nd seat (overcome inertia)
- **Anchoring**: $297 solo price makes team pricing look incredible
- **Enterprise Tier**: 20+ seats = custom pricing, triggers sales conversation

## Behavioral Psychology Applied

### 1. Anchoring Effect (15-20% Revenue Increase)
- **Individual**: $47 Ultimate makes $27 Pro feel like a bargain
- **Business**: $297 solo price anchors all team pricing lower
- **Result**: Customers perceive discounts relative to highest price

### 2. Decoy Pricing (42.8% Revenue Boost)
- **Individual**: Ultimate is the decoy, drives 70% to Pro
- **Business**: 5-9 seat tier pushes customers toward 10+ seats
- **Research**: The Economist study showed 42.8% revenue increase

### 3. Charm Pricing (24% Higher Sales)
- **All prices end in 7 or 9**: $27, $47, $297, $547, $997, $1,747
- **Left-Digit Effect**: $297 feels "under $300"
- **Research**: 24% higher sales than rounded prices

### 4. Loss Aversion & Urgency
- **BETA Pricing**: "Lock in 40% off forever — Prices increase Feb 1, 2025"
- **Countdown Timer**: Creates concrete action trigger
- **Forever Rate Lock**: Fear of missing out drives conversions

### 5. Money-Back Guarantee (26-100% Conversion Lift)
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: 26% conversion increase
- **14-Day Free Trial**: Reduces risk further
- **Consider Testing**: 90-day or 1-year guarantee (doubles conversions)

### 6. Social Proof (34% Increase in Middle Tier Selection)
- **"MOST POPULAR" Badge**: On Pro tier
- **Testimonials**: Specific results ("Saved $45,000")
- **Stats**: "10,000+ developers trained"

### 7. Annual Framing ("2 Months Free" > "16.7% Off")
- **Research**: "Months free" converts 18% better than percentage
- **16.7% Discount**: Most popular in SaaS (equals 2 months free)
- **Target**: 40% annual in Year 1, 60% by Year 3

## Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Risk 1: High Churn
**Impact:** 1% churn change = ±11.6% revenue variance
**Mitigation:**
- Onboarding improvements (reduce time-to-value)
- Proactive outreach at 30/60/90 days
- Usage monitoring and intervention
- Training hour utilization tracking

### Risk 2: Low Conversion
**Impact:** 1% conversion change = ±15.4% revenue variance
**Mitigation:**
- A/B test pricing page continuously
- Optimize free tier (enough value to hook, not enough to stay)
- Improve trial-to-paid conversion (better onboarding)
- Money-back guarantee (test longer periods)

### Risk 3: Competitive Pressure
**Impact:** Competitors may match/undercut pricing
**Mitigation:**
- Differentiate with training (competitors can't easily copy)
- Build switching costs (customer success, integrations)
- Focus on production-ready code quality
- Enterprise features (SSO, compliance)

### Risk 4: Pricing Perception
**Impact:** $27 may be perceived as "too expensive" vs $20 competitors
**Mitigation:**
- Value framing ("$27 = platform + $150 training")
- ROI calculator on pricing page
- Testimonials showing cost savings
- Free tier reduces risk
